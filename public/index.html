The 6 Core Verification Stages

How Pramana catches fraud: 6 clear pipeline stages anyone can verify.

Saying "the AI feels confident" is not good enough for financial safety. Pramana uses AI to read documents and inspect typography, but relies on official government rules, bank clearing registries, and human-readable underwriting memos to decide what is true.

PILLAR 01 • STRUCTURAL EMBED

PAN matches GST Number

By government design, letters 3 to 12 of every 15-character Indian GST number are the exact PAN. Pramana compares them directly — catching mismatched or borrowed PAN cards instantly without needing any internet connection.

PILLAR 02 • CHECKSUM FORMULA

GST Number Math Formula

The 15th digit of every GST number is calculated from the first 14 letters using a government math formula (Modulo-36). Pramana recalculates it instantly — catching fake numbers and typing mistakes on the spot.

Official 15th-Digit Checksum Rule

PILLAR 03 • CLEARING REGISTRY

Live Bank IFSC Search

Pramana checks the IFSC code on the cheque against Razorpay's live national bank directory. It verifies the bank name, branch address, and confirms if NEFT/UPI transfers work. Fake bank codes get caught immediately.
